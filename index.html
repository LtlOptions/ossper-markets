How Ossper works

A quick reference so you know what everything means before you trade.

1. Markets

Each market asks a question about a tournament outcome. Binary markets have YES/NO. Pick-em markets can have 3–20 named outcomes.

2. Prices

A contract price is the current market estimate in virtual dollars. $0.700 means the contract costs about 70¢ per contract before fees and price impact.

3. Buying & selling

Buying spends virtual balance and creates a position. Selling closes some or all of that position. Your confirmation shows the expected debit/credit and estimated P/L.

4. Position status

When trading closes, your position is not deleted. It remains open while the market waits for the official result. Settlement is when the position is finally paid out and closed.

5. Market lifecycle

  • DRAFT: private admin setup.
  • TRADING: users can trade.
  • CLOSED: no new trades.
  • AWAITING RESULT: waiting for the official outcome.
  • SETTLED: positions have been paid and closed.

6. Market depth

Depth controls how strongly a trade moves the price. Higher depth makes the same dollar trade move the market less.

7. Notifications

The bell tells you about important events such as trading closing, a market awaiting a result, and settlement of a position. Unread items are marked with a badge.

8. Freeze system

Admins can temporarily freeze trading. When frozen, viewing and audit history remain available, while trading and market mutations are blocked.

9. Virtual money

Ossper is currently a virtual-money testing environment. Balances and positions are for the community demo and are not real funds.

10. Need help?

Check the Activity and Portfolio pages first. If something still looks wrong, the admin can inspect the audit log and market lifecycle before changing anything.
